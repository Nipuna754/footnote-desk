AERIN HOME - WARRANTY AND RETURNS

Returns
You can return an unused or lightly used purifier within 30 days of delivery
for a full refund.

Return shipping is free within the continental US. Start a return from the
Orders page of your account.

Refunds are paid to the original payment method within 5 working days of the
return arriving at our warehouse.Warranty

The Aerin P3 is covered for 2 years from the date of purchase against
manufacturing defects.

Filters are consumables and are not covered by the warranty.

To make a claim, have your order number and the serial number from the base
of the unit ready. We repair or replace the unit at our choice, and we pay
shipping both ways.

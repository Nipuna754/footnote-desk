AERIN P3 AIR PURIFIER - USER MANUAL

Welcome
Thank you for choosing the Aerin P3. This manual explains how to set up, use
and look after your purifier. Keep it somewhere handy.

What's in the box
The Aerin P3 unit, one HEPA filter (already fitted), one washable pre-filter
(already fitted), a power cable and this manual.

Safety
Use the P3 indoors only. Keep it at least 30 cm from walls and curtains so
air can flow freely. Do not cover the air outlet on top of the unit. Unplug
the unit before cleaning it or changing a filter.Setting up

Remove all packaging, including the plastic bag around the HEPA filter. Open
the filter cover at the back, take the filter out of its bag and push it
back into place until it clicks.

Place the P3 on a flat, firm surface. Plug it in and press the power button.
The ring of lights glows white for three seconds while the air quality
sensor warms up.

Room size
The P3 is designed for rooms up to 40 square metres. In a larger room it
will still work, but it takes longer to clean the air.Fan speeds and night mode

The P3 has four fan speeds. Press the fan button to move through them; the
ring of lights shows the current speed.

Hold the fan button for 3 seconds to turn on night mode. The display dims
and the fan runs at its lowest speed (24 dB).

Auto mode adjusts the speed every few minutes based on the air quality
sensor at the back of the unit. Press the auto button to turn it on or off.Looking after the filters

The P3 uses two filters: a washable pre-filter that catches hair and dust,
and a sealed HEPA filter that catches fine particles.

Replace the HEPA filter every 6 to 8 months. The filter light turns amber
when about two weeks of life remain. After fitting a new filter, hold the
filter button for 5 seconds to reset the light.

Do not wash the HEPA filter; water damages the fibres. The pre-filter can be
vacuumed or rinsed every two weeks and must be fully dry before refitting.Troubleshooting

If the unit clicks but the fan does not start, check that the filter cover
is fully closed. A safety switch stops the fan while the cover is open.

If the display shows E2, unplug the unit for 30 seconds and plug it back in.
If E2 returns, contact support with the serial number from the base of the
unit.

If the air smells musty, the pre-filter may be damp. Take it out and let it
dry completely before refitting.Specifications

Model: Aerin P3. Power: 220-240 V, 35 W at maximum speed. Noise: 24 dB
(night mode) to 52 dB (maximum speed). Weight: 4.1 kg. Dimensions: 22 x 22 x
48 cm.
